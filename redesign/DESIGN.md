## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the calculated precision of an haute-horlogerie atelier crossed with high-performance digital systems architecture. Designed for an elite senior software engineer and digital architect, the visual language departs entirely from cliché developer portfolios, hyperactive neon gradients, and cookie-cutter SaaS templates. Instead, it projects quiet authority, intellectual rigor, architectural permanence, and understated luxury.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on:
- **Swiss Editorial Rigor:** Asymmetric grid foundations, stark typographical contrasts, micro-indices, Roman numeral structural indexing, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Stealth Luxury Tech:** Deep, inky obsidian canvasses, multi-layered slate graphite surface planes, razor-thin translucent borders (1px hairline), and extremely disciplined, surgical strikes of luminescent emerald.
- **Architectural Materiality:** Subtle frosted glass finishes, monochromatic tactile surfaces, and precise monospaced metadata markers that communicate systems mastery without visual noise.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is strictly calibrated for dark-first, low-reflectance, high-legibility environments. Deep Obsidian (`#090A0F`) forms the infinite negative canvas. Depth is established through incremental lifts into Slate Graphite tonal planes (`#0E1017` through `#1C202E`), avoiding stark contrasts in favor of subtle spatial shifts.

Hairline borders (`#272C3D` and translucent white overlays) delineate boundaries like mechanical watch bevels. The accent tone—a cold, luminous emerald (`#10B981` / `#34D399`)—is utilized with severe restraint: reserved exclusively for interactive active states, critical telemetry signals, live deployment indicators, and precise terminal focal points. Text is never harsh pure white; instead, warm platinum (`#F1F5F9`) and graded slates deliver effortless reading comfort.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes structural sculptured forms with exacting technical legibility.

- **Syne (Display & Primary Headlines):** Provides unconventional, high-character geometric distinction. Its wide proportions and distinctive glyph tension evoke bespoke editorial catalog design.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Secondary Structure):** Delivers clean, neutral, and readable architectural pacing for technical case studies and architectural overviews.
- **JetBrains Mono (Technical Metadata & Indices):** Governs section numbering (e.g., `// 01.0 ARCHITECTURE`, `SEC.IV`), telemetry metrics, inline code snippets, and interface coordinates.

All section headings pair a micro-index prefix set in uppercase monospaced text with an editorial title. Letter spacing is tightened on large display sizes and widened on labels and monospaced indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid with deliberate breathing room and high negative space ratio. Sections transition through generous vertical rhythms (`space-2xl` and beyond) rather than cramped dividers.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column asymmetric layout; outer margin of `5rem` (`margin-desktop`) with `2.5rem` gutters. Section titles often occupy a dedicated 3- or 4-column left rail, locking the structural context while content cascades across the remaining 8 or 9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout; outer margin of `3rem` (`margin-tablet`) with `1.5rem` gutters. Left rail titles transition smoothly above section content blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout; outer margin of `1.5rem` (`margin`). Columns collapse vertically, preserving monospaced index headers at the top of each block to maintain high-end structural continuity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft colored drop-shadows or playful elevation tiers. Depth is achieved purely through precise light management:

- **Surface Tiers:** Background begins at `#090A0F`. Base cards use `#151822`. Interactive or elevated states transition to `#1C202E`.
- **Satin Glass Surfaces:** Modals, navigation bars, and top-layer floating panels employ backdrop-filter blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`) set over semi-transparent slate foundations (`rgba(21, 24, 34, 0.75)`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Every elevated surface is enclosed with an ultra-fine 1px border using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#272C3D`. On hover, borders gently transition to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` or an emerald-tinted ghost stroke (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).
- **Subtle Radial Occlusion:** Key container backgrounds may integrate a restrained, large-radius (600px+) radial gradient that casts a soft, sub-perceptual dark emerald luminance (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.03)`) to ground high-priority architectural diagrams.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly soft-architectural (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor disciplined, precision-machined corners over casual bulbous curves.

- **Micro elements & tags:** 2px corner radius (`0.125rem`).
- **Standard UI elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** 4px corner radius (`0.25rem`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** 6px to 8px corner radius (`0.375rem` - `0.5rem`).
- **Circular Elements:** Reserved exclusively for status indicators (pulsing 6px nodes) and avatar/icon enclosures. 

No generic rounded-pill badge bubbles are permitted anywhere in the system.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid warm platinum background (`#F1F5F9`) with ink-black typography (`#090A0F`), font weight 600, 4px corner radius. On hover, subtly dims to `#E2E8F0` with a sharp 1px offset ring.
- **Secondary / Technical Action:** Graphite surface (`#151822`) with hairline border (`#272C3D`) and platinum text (`#F1F5F9`). On hover, border activates to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with a faint interior emerald wash (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`).
- **Text / Index Action:** Monospaced label (`JetBrains Mono`) with an arrow glyph (`→`) that shifts horizontally by 4px on hover, accompanied by a subtle underline animation.

### Structural Tags & Technology Micro-Indices
- Generic pill tags are forbidden. Technical capabilities and stack items are rendered as minimal, un-pill-like brackets or crisp rectilinear tokens.
- Styling: Flat background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), 1px border (`#272C3D`), 2px radius, monospace typography (`label-code`), prefixed with an unobtrusive terminal slash (e.g., `/ KUBERNETES`, `/ RUST`).

### Cards & Project Architecture Display
- **Container:** Dark slate `#151822` base with a 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` border and 6px radius.
- **Header:** Features a top technical ledger containing the project index (e.g., `ARCH.04 // 2024`) in JetBrains Mono muted text (`#64748B`), opposite an active status indicator (a 6px emerald dot with a faint 2px ping).
- **Body & Content:** High-contrast Syne title, followed by precise, generous-height editorial description.
- **Hover Behavior:** No dramatic vertical translation. Border color sharpens subtly to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` with a gentle top-down satin gradient shimmer.

### Input Fields & Terminal Consoles
- **Field Base:** Recessed `#0E1017` background with a crisp `#272C3D` perimeter.
- **Focus State:** 1px border transitions directly to the primary emerald accent (`#10B981`) with zero diffuse outer glow rings, preserving clean edge definition.
- **Typography:** JetBrains Mono for inputs and forms, reinforcing architectural calculation and user intentionality.

### Lists & Case Study Indexes
- Border-separated ledger rows (`1px solid #1C202E`).
- Each row displays an index counter (`01`, `02`, `03`) in muted monospace, an editorial project title in Syne, an architectural role column, and a discreet contextual metadata stamp.
- Row hover produces a soft, uniform background illumination (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`) across the full width.
